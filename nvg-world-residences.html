An ecosystem in motion

North Bengaluru isn’t just growing. It’s changing.

The city’s centre of gravity is steadily expanding northwards, driven by an ecosystem of airport infrastructure, business districts, aerospace and industrial development, new mobility infrastructure and residential growth.

The numbers tell the story.

44.47 MN

Passengers handled by Kempegowda International Airport in FY2025–26 (BIAL).

58.19 KM

Approximate length of the Namma Metro Blue Line connecting the city towards Kempegowda International Airport.

~2,000 ACRES

144 KM

New Yelahanka Town

Within North Bengaluru, some addresses already have a head start.

While North Bengaluru represents the city’s next wave of growth, New Yelahanka Town represents an established residential ecosystem within it.

Planned as a residential township in the northern part of Bengaluru, Yelahanka New Town has grown into a mature neighbourhood with established residential communities, schools, healthcare, retail and everyday conveniences.

Established. Connected. Evolving.

ESTABLISHED COMMUNITY

A developed residential neighbourhood with an existing social and civic ecosystem.

STRATEGIC ROAD CONNECTIVITY

Connected through Bellary Road / NH-44 and SH-9, with access towards the airport corridor and the wider city.

RAIL & TRANSIT ACCESS

Yelahanka Junction provides rail connectivity, while future transit plans continue to strengthen the broader northern corridor.

PLANNED FUTURE GROWTH

Planning proposals for the Yelahanka district include further transit integration, network development and urban densification.
